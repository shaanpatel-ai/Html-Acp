Soccer






About Soccer

Soccer, also known globally as association football, is the world's most popular sport, played by two teams of 11 players on a rectangular grass field with the main goal of scoring more points than the opponent.


History Of Soccer

Soccer traces its roots from ancient kicking games in China to codified rules in 19th-century England, key milestones including ancient cuju, medieval mob football, and the 1863 formation of The Football Association.
