About Ms Dhoni

Mahendra Singh Dhoni (born July 7, 1981) is a legendary Indian cricketer and captain widely regarded as one of the greatest finishers and wicket-keeper batsmen in history. Known as "Captain Cool," he is the only captain to win all three major ICC trophies: the 2007 T20 World Cup, 2011 ODI World Cup, and 2013 Champions Trophy.




About Sachin Tendulkar

Sachin Tendulkar, fondly known as the "God of Cricket," is one of the greatest cricketers in the history of the sport. Born on April 24, 1973, in Mumbai, India, he displayed extraordinary talent and passion for cricket from a young age. Over his illustrious career spanning more than two decades, Tendulkar became a global icon, admired for his impeccable batting skills, consistency, and sportsmanship. With numerous records, including being the first cricketer to score 100 international centuries.
