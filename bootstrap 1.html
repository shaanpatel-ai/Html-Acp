About Ms Dhoni

Mahendra Singh Dhoni (born July 7, 1981) is a legendary Indian cricketer and captain widely regarded as one of the greatest finishers and wicket-keeper batsmen in history. Known as "Captain Cool," he is the only captain to win all three major ICC trophies: the 2007 T20 World Cup, 2011 ODI World Cup, and 2013 Champions Trophy.
