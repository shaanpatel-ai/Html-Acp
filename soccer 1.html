About Soccer

Soccer (or football) is the world's most popular sport, featuring two teams of 11 players aiming to score by maneuvering a ball into the opponent's goal, primarily using their feet. Governed by FIFA, this 90-minute game is played by ~250 million people in over 200 countries, with major tournaments like the World Cup.
